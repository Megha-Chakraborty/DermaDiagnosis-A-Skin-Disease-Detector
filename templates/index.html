Understanding Skin Cancer

Early detection of skin cancer significantly improves treatment outcomes. Understanding the causes and taking preventive measures can protect your skin health. Our system helps identify potential concerns, but always consult a dermatologist for professional diagnosis.

🔬 What Causes Skin Cancer?

Key factors that increase risk:

☀️ UV Radiation Exposure

Primary cause - prolonged sun exposure and tanning beds damage skin cell DNA

👤 Fair Skin & Genetics

Less melanin provides less UV protection; family history increases risk

🔴 Atypical Moles

Having many moles (50+) or irregular moles increases melanoma risk

🧬 Weakened Immune System

Immunosuppression, organ transplants, or HIV increases susceptibility

🔄 Previous Skin Cancer

History of skin cancer significantly increases risk of recurrence or new lesions

⚗️ Chemical & Environmental

Exposure to arsenic, coal tar, or previous radiation therapy

🛡️ Prevention & Precautions

Protect your skin with these steps:

🧴 Daily Sun Protection
  • Use broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily
  • Reapply every 2 hours when outdoors
  • Wear protective clothing, hats, and sunglasses
🚫 Avoid Harmful Exposure
  • Avoid tanning beds and sun lamps completely
  • Seek shade during peak sun hours (10 AM - 4 PM)
  • Be extra careful near water, sand, and snow
🔍 Regular Self-Examinations
  • Check your skin monthly using the ABCDE rule
  • Look for new moles or changes in existing ones
  • Use a mirror to check hard-to-see areas
👨‍⚕️ Professional Screening
  • Annual dermatologist checkups recommended
  • Immediate consultation for suspicious changes
  • Early detection saves lives - don't delay!

About this Project

This project uses machine learning to predict skin disorders, with a focus on melanoma detection. The model was trained on the HAM10000 dataset and applies feature engineering techniques to improve accuracy.

Features analyzed include demographic information, lesion characteristics, and medical history to provide a comprehensive assessment.
